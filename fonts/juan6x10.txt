;;
;; Each glyph is drawn 5 pixels wide (X = black). The tool adds a blank column
;; on the right and a blank row on top, so a glyph may have up to 9 rows:
;; rows 1-7 are the capital height, rows 8-9 are for descenders.
;; Missing rows are blank. A glyph starts with "= " and the character
;; ("= space" for the space character).
;;
;; A glyph drawn 6 wide fills the whole cell instead: up to 10 rows, starting
;; at the cell's top row. Line drawing and accented capitals are drawn so.
;;
;; Which character gets which code is in tools/model/charset.py.
